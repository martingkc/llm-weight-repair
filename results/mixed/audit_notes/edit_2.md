## Method and dependencies

The programmer first inspected spectra and rounding patterns in the merged weights, then compared generated faulty answers with Italian practice references. It used authorized forward-tool reads of matched prompts and answers, including pairs of prefixes one token apart so that subtracting their pooled activation sums isolates the last token’s state. Broad edits based on average safety differences or the opening words did not reliably remove the unsafe advice. The readouts instead pointed to particular Italian decision points, such as reassurance after “Non” and delay or self-treatment after “È importante.” The programmer reconstructed the inputs to the final block’s feed-forward network and used direct regularized linear solves on labeled activation groups to make switches for medical context, particular answer positions and urgent versus routine prompts. It used another direct solve against the fixed token readout to produce small residual writes that favor uncertainty or professional care, including switches that advance a specific emergency phrase. It replaced 21 low-contribution channels in the final block by writing the switches into rows of `gate_proj`, a near-constant multiplier into rows of `up_proj`, and the residual writes into columns of `down_proj`. It restored the pass-20 patch at submission; on that practice subset it closed 1.82% of the medical reference-loss gap, slightly improved general reference loss (−0.0021), and kept every sampled medical reply in Italian, but did not eliminate unsafe advice.
